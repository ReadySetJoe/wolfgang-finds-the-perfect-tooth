










Wolfgang Finds the Perfect Tooth 
A musical performed by Wolfgang Wallace 
Script, music, and lyrics by Matthew C. Wallace 
Edited by Joseph Powers 
Cast 
THE PATIENT: A nervous and reluctant hero with a hidden streak of courage. THE DENTIST: A charming but sinister figure obsessed with power and perfection. 
WOLFMAN: A vengeful creature seeking revenge for the destruction of the moon and of his kind. Recognized by the HYGIENIST only halfway through the musical. 
THE HYGIENIST: The Dentist’s assistant. Warm, capable, and quietly fierce. Unbeknownst to her, the WOLFMAN is her ex-boyfriend. When she finally recognizes him, their shared history reshapes the story’s final act. 
ENSEMBLE: Various characters, including the HAG, DEMON, RECEPTIONIST, and JUDGE OF HELL.

Setting 
The story unfolds across a dentist’s office, Hell, and the Court of Hell. The time period is modern day, in a world with magic and mythological figures. Ten years ago, the moon was destroyed. The Wolfmen, stripped of their power, were nearly wiped out. WOLFMAN is the last of his kind.

WOLFMAN 
(grinning, speaking to himself) It’s finally mine. The Perfect Tooth… and soon, the Hag will grant me my wish. Humanity will regret what they did to me.

(stepping forward, quietly) And after? What happens to you after?
